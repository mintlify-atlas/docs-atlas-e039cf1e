Auditorio Estelar is an Android application that lets users browse upcoming concerts at a stellar auditorium, pick their seats on an interactive seating plan, and complete their ticket purchase — all from their phone. The app communicates with a PHP REST API backed by a MySQL database to fetch real-time concert listings, session schedules, and seat availability.

## How it works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create an account">
    Register with your name, email, and password. Passwords are hashed with PHP's `password_hash` before storage.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Browse the billboard">
    The home screen shows all active concerts fetched from the backend, each with a promotional poster loaded via Glide.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a session and seats">
    Tap a concert to see upcoming sessions. Then pick your seats on the interactive seating map — available, selected, and reserved states are shown in real time.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm your reservation">
    Review your selected seats and total price, then confirm. The backend processes the reservation atomically, preventing double-booking.
  </Step>
</Steps>
